# 💬 Blog Comments & Discussions

This repository serves as the discussion backend for comments and reactions on my personal blog and portfolio: [sakhiledumisa.com](https://sakhiledumisa.com) (or your portfolio domain).

Powered by **[Giscus](https://giscus.app)** and **GitHub Discussions**.

## 📖 How It Works

- Each article on the blog automatically maps to a corresponding discussion thread in this repository's **Discussions** section under the **Announcements** category.
- Comments, replies, and emoji reactions left on the blog are stored directly as GitHub Discussions here.
- Readers can comment by signing in with their GitHub accounts.

## 🛡️ Community Guidelines

All discussions and comments must adhere to standard community rules:
- **Be respectful and constructive**: Harassment, spam, or hate speech will be deleted immediately.
- **Stay on topic**: Keep comments relevant to the specific article being discussed.
- **No unsolicited advertising or spam links**.
